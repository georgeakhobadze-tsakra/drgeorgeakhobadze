FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
Who is this Academy designed for? +
The Academy is designed for medical students, residents, and healthcare professionals who want to deepen their understanding of core medical sciences through the lens of real pediatric intensive care experience.
When will the courses be available? +
The first course modules are being recorded and will launch to subscribers first. By joining now, you'll be among the first to access all content when it goes live and help shape the curriculum.
Can I cancel my subscription? +
Yes. Monthly subscribers can cancel at any time, and the subscription remains active until the end of the billing period. Annual subscribers can cancel renewal before the year ends. Content you've enrolled in remains accessible.
Are lectures available in Georgian? +
Yes. All video lectures include both Georgian and English subtitles. The instruction itself is conducted in a way that is accessible to Georgian-language learners, with terminology explained in both languages.
What makes this different from other medical education platforms? +
Every topic is taught by Dr. George Akhobadze, drawing directly from 14+ years of pediatric ICU practice. The curriculum bridges theoretical knowledge with clinical decision-making in a way that standard textbooks and generic platforms cannot replicate.
