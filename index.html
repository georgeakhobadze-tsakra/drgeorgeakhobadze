About the Doctor
Dr. George
Akhobadze
MD · Pediatric Anesthesiologist & Critical Care Specialist

With over 14 years of dedicated clinical practice, Dr. Akhobadze has established himself as a leading specialist in pediatric anesthesiology and critical care. His expertise spans some of the most complex and delicate medical procedures — including neonatal and congenital heart surgery — where precision and calm under pressure are paramount. Beyond the operating room, he leads three hospital departments and is passionate about making medical knowledge accessible to both aspiring physicians and the families who need it most.
